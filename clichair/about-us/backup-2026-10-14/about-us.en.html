The origin

Distribution first. Then the platform.

cliCHair started where two trades that rarely talk to each other met: a master colorist with years of technical work behind the chair, who knows the real limits of a product because she has used it on her clients' hair, and a developer convinced that a salon's decisions could be calculated rather than guessed.

ÜLTIMA hair concept was founded in 2018, and for years we worked as a professional distributor in the classic way: salon visits, samples, printed price lists. That period taught us the trade from the inside, and showed us precisely which parts of the job were real expertise and which were simply repetition.

cliCHair is the next step. We automated the repetition and kept the expertise. The method did not come after the technology: the technology was built to run the method.

The platform

Always open, between the brand and the people who use it

cliCHair is the Swiss platform dedicated to professional haircare. We are the official importer of the brands we carry, with fast delivery throughout Switzerland.

We work as a hub: the brand on one side, the people who use the product on the other, and in between a platform that never closes. No appointment to arrange, no round of phone calls, no waiting for a price list. You order when you need to, with prices already set for your profile and technical calculation available at any time.

Everything that usually takes a phone call or an email lives inside your account: orders, reorders, invoices, tracking, returns, purchase history. No office hours to work around, no form to fill in twice. An automated process costs less, wastes no paper and adds no unnecessary steps: this is where both the pricing further down this page and the way we ship come from.

Every type of access is verified and sees the catalogue that belongs to it. Private clients find the retail selection: the same products a hairdresser recommends and sells in the salon, with the same INCI lists and the same technical advice. Verified professionals also reach the full catalogue, with the technical lines, the colour ranges and the professional formats that stay out of sight for anyone outside the trade.

The principle

Depth, not breadth

The difference is not how many products you find. It is how well we know them.

We work with a small number of complete professional ranges, technical and colour lines included, studied down to the last INCI. A generalist assortment offers you a few retail references. The engine

clichAIr

It is not a support chat. It is the calculation engine the platform is named after.

If you run a salon, it works out dosages per application, cost per service, how many applications you get from one pack and how profitable a kit is. If you are buying for yourself, it does the opposite job and an equally useful one: it reads the INCI lists, checks that the products you are putting together are compatible and builds the right routine for your hair, so you stop buying by trial and error.

It answers 24 hours a day, on the site and on the messaging channels you already use. And when it recognises that a question goes beyond its limits, it passes you to the right person. Click the AIr badge, bottom left: it is on every page of the site.

The validation

AI does not decide on its own

Every formula goes through our team of experts, every protocol through technical verification in the field. Nothing enters the platform without that check.

The platform does not learn on its own and does not learn from the web: it grows only on what the team has verified.
